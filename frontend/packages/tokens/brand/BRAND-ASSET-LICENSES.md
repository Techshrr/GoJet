## External asset rule

No third-party brand mark is bundled by P02. Before any later external brand
asset is committed, its record must name the canonical upstream source,
license/terms URL or bundled license identifier, retrieval date, whether
recoloring is permitted, and any required attribution. Source priority is:

1. Official Brand Kit
2. Official SVG
3. Simple Icons

Unrecorded stock imagery, watermarked imagery, scraped UI screenshots and
fabricated product screenshots are prohibited.
